# Scam Checker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Scam Checker is a free app that helps you spot scam texts, emails, and calls. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does with information. The short version: **the app collects nothing, sends nothing about you anywhere, and has no accounts.**

## What you paste into the checker

Messages you paste or type into the checker are analyzed **on your phone only**. They are never uploaded, transmitted, stored, or shared. When you close the app, nothing about them remains anywhere except in the app's own screen while you are looking at it.

## Data the app downloads

About once per app open, the app downloads a small data file (the current scam-pattern list) from a public file host (GitHub). This is a one-way download of public information. The app sends no personal information with the request; the host's servers will see a standard technical request (such as an IP address) the same way any web server does when a file is downloaded. GitHub's own privacy policy covers its server logs.

## Advertising

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob on some screens (never on the emergency-help screens). AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers and use them to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's advertising policies. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Partner offers

After a scan, the app may show an optional offer from an identity-protection partner (such as LifeLock). If you tap it, your browser opens the partner's website and that site's own privacy policy applies. The app does not share anything about you or your scans with the partner. We may earn a commission if you sign up, at no cost to you, and the offer is labeled as a partner link in the app.

## What the app does NOT do

- No user accounts, sign-ups, or logins
- No analytics or usage tracking by the developer
- No collection of your messages, contacts, location, or files
- No selling or sharing of personal information (we have none to sell)

## Permissions

The app requests one permission: internet access, used only to download updated scam lists and to load ads.

## Children

The app is a general-audience safety tool and is not directed at children under 13.
